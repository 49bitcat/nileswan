---
title: 'Updating firmware'
weight: 10
---

It is recommended to use the latest nileswan firmware. To update your firmware, proceed as follows:

1. Ensure your console's battery is fully charged. SPI and MCU flashing operations may cause brief periods of increased power draw.
2. Download the latest firmware update version from [here](https://github.com/49bitcat/nileswan/releases/latest/download/nileswan-fw-update.ws).
3. Launch the `.ws` file.
4. After reviewing the messages, press `A` (or `Circle` on PCv2) to install.
5. Wait for the installation to complete. **Do not turn off the console** until the updater tells you it is safe to do so.
